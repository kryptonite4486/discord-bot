Your alliance's stats, from screenshots to leaderboards

LastZ Assistant is a Discord bot for Last Z alliances. Upload the in-game leaderboard and member screenshots you already take, and it keeps each player's weekly numbers and turns them into leaderboards, trends and growth reports for your server.

Join the support server

Opening to new servers soon. Ask in the support server to be told when it does.

How it works

  1. Upload screenshots. Run /ingest image in your alliance's channel, pick what the screenshots show (Versus Points, Tech Contribution, Power, Kills…) and attach them.
  2. The bot reads them. It reads each player's name and number from the image and saves them for that week. Numbers that look wrong, such as a player's Power dropping sharply, are flagged for you to check.
  3. Ask for a report. /report gives a weekly summary, leaderboards, a player's history, trends across weeks and who grew the most, as text or charts.

What it tracks

Versus Points, Tech Contribution, HQ level, Power, Arena Power and Kills, every week, for every player. Missed a screenshot? Add a single number with /add or paste a list with /ingest text.

Trivia for your members

Between events, run a trivia match in your server, or open it to every server running the bot and compete against other alliances. Questions range from Last Z itself to general knowledge, with a leaderboard for each server and across servers.

Also included

  • A link to the territory planner with /planner.
  • Separate datasets per channel, so one server can track several groups.
  • Tools to merge a player stored under two spellings, and to export your data as CSV or JSON.
  • Data deletion whenever you want it, and automatic deletion 30 days after the bot is removed. See the Privacy Policy.

Setup

  1. Add the bot to your server with the Add to Discord button (available soon). It asks only for Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Read Message History, and it can't read your server's ordinary messages.
  2. Choose your channels with /setup (Manage Server): a trivia channel to keep matches out of your stats channels, and a report channel for the bot's notices.
  3. Upload your first screenshots with /ingest image in the channel that will hold your alliance's stats.
  4. Run a report, such as /report week or /report leaderboard. /help lists every command.

Questions

Can the bot read my server's messages?

No. It uses no privileged intents, so it only sees slash commands, the screenshots you attach to them, and messages that @mention it during a /ingest batch.

Who can see our alliance's data?

Only your server. Data is kept per server and per channel, and reports never mix servers. Cross-server trivia shows other players only your server's name, your members' display names and their trivia scores.

What if a screenshot is read wrong?

The bot flags numbers that look implausible for that player, and you can correct any value with /add.

Is this made by the Last Z developers?

No. It's an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Last Z or by Discord.
