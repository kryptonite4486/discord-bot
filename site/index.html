LastZ Assistant

A Discord bot for Last Z alliances. It reads leaderboard and member screenshots, keeps weekly stats for each player (Versus Points, Tech Contribution, HQ level, Power, Arena Power and Kills), and turns them into leaderboards, trends and growth reports for your server.

It also links to the territory planner.
