Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This policy explains what the LastZ Assistant Discord bot (“the bot”) stores, why, for how long, and how to get it deleted. LastZ Assistant (“we”, “us”) runs the bot. Contact us at lastzassistant@gmail.com.

What the bot stores

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
In-game player names and stats: Versus Points, Tech Contribution, HQ level, Power, Arena Power and Kills, by week Server members add them with commands, pasted text or game screenshots To produce the server's leaderboards, trends and reports
Discord server and channel IDs Discord, with each command To keep each server's and channel's data separate
Trivia scores: for each person who answers a trivia question, their Discord user ID, display name and server name, with their points, answers, games and wins Discord, when that person presses an answer button To show match results and trivia leaderboards
Usage counts: per server per day, the number of screenshots processed, failures, and processing and waiting time The bot, as it works Capacity planning, fair queuing and usage limits. No player data.
Subscription records: which server, which plan (paid once subscriptions are offered, gifted, or a free trial), start and end dates, and the Discord user ID of whoever redeemed a gift code or started a trial Discord, or us for gifted plans; the bot when a server admin starts a free trial or redeems a code To provide paid features, and to keep the free trial to one per server. We never see payment details: Discord handles payments.
Operational logs: commands run, errors, and processing details. These can include the Discord username and ID of the person who ran an admin command, player names, and uploaded file names. The bot, as it works To fix problems and investigate misuse

Screenshots aren't kept. Images and zip files you upload are downloaded, read by a vision model running on our own hardware (not a third-party AI service), and discarded once the stats are extracted. They aren't used to train any model.

The bot can't read your messages. It doesn't have Discord's Message Content permission, so Discord only shows it the text and attachments of messages that @mention it. It works through slash commands, and it doesn't store message text, member lists or Discord account details, apart from the trivia scores of people who choose to play trivia. While someone runs /ingest batch, the bot uses only that person's messages that @mention it in that channel, to collect their screenshots, until they send done.

About player names

Player names come from the game and are uploaded by alliance members, usually the alliance's leaders. That means the bot can hold names of players who have never used it. If your in-game name is stored and you want it removed, ask the server's admins, who can delete it with /data delete, or contact us with the server and name, and we'll remove it.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the bot's features to the server it was added in. Each server's data is kept separate and is never shown to another server, with one exception: in a cross-server trivia match, which members choose to join, players' display names, scores and server names are shown to the other servers in that match and on the cross-server trivia leaderboard. Server admins can turn cross-server trivia off with /trivia settings, which also removes the server from that leaderboard. We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

How long it's kept

SituationWhat happens
The bot is in your serverYour server's data is kept so reports can show history.
A server admin runs /data deleteThat channel's or the whole server's data is deleted immediately.
A server admin runs /trivia resetThe server's trivia scores are deleted immediately.
The bot is removed from your serverData is kept for 30 days, then deleted. Adding the bot back within 30 days keeps everything, so removing it by accident loses nothing.
The bot is removed, but the server has an active subscriptionData is kept while the subscription is active, then for 30 days after it ends.
BackupsKept for at most 30 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within 30 days of being deleted.
Usage countsKept, because they contain no player data.
Subscription recordsKept, including after /data delete or the bot's removal, as a record of what each server was entitled to. They contain no player data.
Free trial recordThe server's ID and the date its free trial started are kept even after /data delete or the bot's removal, so each server gets only one trial.
Operational logsKept in size-limited files that are overwritten as new entries are written.

Where it's stored

The bot and its database run on our own computer in Pennsylvania, United States. Backups are kept on our own storage, which may be synced to our personal cloud storage (for example Apple iCloud). Discord processes your commands and uploads under Discord's Privacy Policy. The website you're reading is hosted by Cloudflare.

Your choices and rights

  • Server admins can delete their server's data at any time with /data delete, for one channel or the whole server.
  • Server admins can delete their server's trivia scores at any time with /trivia reset.
  • Anyone can contact us to ask what's stored about an in-game name or their trivia scores, or to have it corrected or deleted.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority. We rely on our legitimate interest in providing the features a server's admins asked for.

Age

You must meet Discord's minimum age for your country to use the bot.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data, including keeping it on our own hardware and limiting who can run admin commands. No system is perfectly secure, though, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we'll also tell servers through the bot or our support channels before it takes effect.
